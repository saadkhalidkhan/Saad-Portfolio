Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

Welcome to Mizafi, a personal finance tracking application developed by Saad Product Labs. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To help you track your budget smoothly, the app processes the following information:

  • Financial Data: Income amounts, expense details, transaction categories, and monthly balances that you manually input into the app.
  • Account Data (Future Layer): If you choose to use cloud synchronization features in future updates, we may collect authentication identifiers (such as email or Google sign-in details) to secure your profile.

2. How Your Data is Stored

  • Local Storage: By default, all financial data you enter into Mizafi is stored strictly on your local device. We do not have access to it.
  • Cloud Synchronization: If cloud backup features are enabled by you, your transaction details are encrypted and securely synchronized using industry-standard cloud infrastructure (Firebase).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data strictly to provide the app's core functionalities, including:

  • Generating local financial analytics, charts, and budget summaries.
  • Providing automated insights into your financial habits.
  • Maintaining data backups across your authorized devices.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Parties

We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal or financial data to third parties. Your data is solely used to power your personal app experience. We do not integrate with predatory loan systems, cryptocurrency exchanges, or unverified advertising brokers.

5. User Control and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete individual transactions or clear the entire app storage directly from your device settings at any time. If you use our cloud sync feature, a dedicated button inside the app will allow you to purge your account and delete all remote backups permanently from our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at your professional communication channels provided in our app developer profile.
